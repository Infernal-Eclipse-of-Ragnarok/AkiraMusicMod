Replaces some of the music in the game with some custom tracks from various artists.
Each track replacement is configurable.

The follwoing themes are replaced:
[list]
	[*] Forest Day (Weavers - Sakuzyo)
	[*] Forest Night (Unconscious - Sakuzyo)
	[*] Jungle (Marshland - Sakuzyo)
	[*] Evil Surface (Rotten Forest - Sakuzyo)
	[*] Space (Floating - Sakuzyo)
	[*] Town Day (Safe Haven - Yoko Shimomura)
	[*] Town Night (Safe Haven - Orvynx ft. Yoko Shimomura)
	[*] Boss 1 (Instinct - Sakuzyo)
	[*] Mech Bosses (War Without Reason - Heaven Pierce Her)
	[*] Plantera (Neurotoxin - Sakuzyo)
	[*] Jungle Temple (Star Of The Show - NoLongerNull)
	[*] Duke Fishron (Sewer Creature - NoLongerNull)
	[*] Lunar Pillars (Dark Fountain (Titan Spawn) - fluffyhairs & Toby Fox)
	[*] Moon Lord (GAURDIAN - Toby Fox)
[/list]

The following themes are added: 
[list]
	[*] Infernal Eclipse of Ragnarok Menu (Teardrops of Dragonfire - Peter Fung)
	[*] Mutant Interlude (Axion - Sakuzyo)
	[*] Soul of Eternity Interlude (End of Story - Sakuzyo)
[/list]

This mod also adds the Infernal Eclipse of Ragnarok menu theme by it self if you don't have it on.
